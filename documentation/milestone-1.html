WHAT TO KNOW BEFORE GETTING A DOGGIE!

Purpose, Audience & Goal

Purpose:

This project will be a website about dogs. Helps people choose the right breed, prepare for a new dog, and learn the basics of dog care. It will also serve as a showcase of my front-end web development skills.

Audience:

This site is designed for people who are thinking about getting a dog or who have just brought one home. The target audience includes first-time dog owners, families with children, people considering adoption from a shelter or rescue, and dog lovers who want practical, trustworthy advice.

Goal:

The primary goal of this site is for visitors to quickly find a breed or care guide that fits their lifestyle. To feel confident about their next step, whether that is contacting a local shelter, saving a checklist, or reaching out to me through the contact form. The secondary goal is to demonstrate clean, responsive, accessible front-end design.

Persona

Name: Maya Sanchez

Age: 27

Profession:

Graphic designer at a small marketing agency. She works a hybrid schedule, with three days in the office and two at home.

Technology:

Uses an iPhone for most browsing and a laptop for work. She reads reviews and guides on her phone during her commute and lunch breaks, so pages need to load fast and look good on a small screen.

Attitudes and Behaviors:

Maya is thoughtful and a little anxious about making the wrong choice. She wants a dog that fits her apartment and her schedule, and she reads several sources before making a decision. She skims headings and images first and will leave a site that feels cluttered, outdated, or full of pop-ups. She trusts sites that look clean and give clear, specific answers.

Frustrations and Needs:

Maya has never owned a dog and is overwhelmed by conflicting advice online. Breed lists are often long and hard to compare, and many sites are cluttered with ads. She needs simple comparisons of size, energy level, grooming, and cost, along with a beginner-friendly checklist of supplies and first-week steps. She also wants to know what a dog realistically costs each month so she can budget before adopting.

Goals:

Maya wants to know whether a particular type of dog is a good match for her apartment, work schedule, and budget. Within a couple of minutes on the site, she wants to narrow her options to two or three breeds and know what to do next. She wants to feel prepared, not pressured.

User Story:

As a first-time dog owner, I need to quickly find which dogs fit my lifestyle and what I need to prepare, so that I can make a confident decision about adopting.
